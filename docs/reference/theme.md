# Theme feature

`toc.integrate` is a MkDocs Material feature. This page is the short reference for what it changes.

## What it keeps

The site map from `nav` stays in the left navigation. Heading permalinks in the article stay. Search stays.

## What it moves

Level-two and level-three headings on the current page are listed under that page in the left navigation.

## What it removes

The secondary sidebar, which IDP shows on the right of a normal docs page, is not rendered.

## What it does not change

The IDP reader stylesheet still lays the article out as if a right sidebar existed. You may see empty space on the right. That space is the reader, not MkDocs.
